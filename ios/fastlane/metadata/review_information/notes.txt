The app transcribes speech on the device with Qwen3-ASR 1.7B (MLX, on the GPU) and makes no network requests. Tap the microphone button and speak Cantonese (or play a Cantonese video near the phone); captions appear while you speak and refresh as often as the phone keeps up (at most every 0.5 s); every few seconds a line is finished. Loading the model takes a few seconds on the first tap. iOS does not allow GPU work in the background, so leaving the app stops listening. No account is needed. The app needs an iPhone with 8 GB of memory (iPhone 15 Pro or later); please review it on one.
