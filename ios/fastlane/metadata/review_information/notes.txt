The app transcribes speech entirely on the device (Silero VAD and SenseVoice-Small on the CPU, Qwen3-ASR 1.7B with MLX on the GPU) and makes no network requests. No account is needed.

To test: tap the microphone button and speak Cantonese, or play Cantonese audio near the phone. A sample conversation in Cantonese, English and Putonghua is at https://github.com/DE0CH/cantonese-live-ios/raw/main/testdata/demo/demo-16k.wav (play it from another device). Captions appear in grey while someone speaks and turn black at each pause. Loading the models takes a few seconds on the first tap.

The interface follows the phone's language (Cantonese, Traditional Chinese, Simplified Chinese or English); captions are always in Hong Kong Traditional Chinese. iOS does not allow GPU work in the background, so leaving the app stops listening. The app needs an iPhone with 8 GB of memory (iPhone 15 Pro or later).
